Delays are a part of life. Delaying something by a few weeks isn't the end of the world. Your audience will be there, just as eager as before to learn from you.

It's unfortunate that unfortunate things tend to happen frequently enough.

Delay, but do not give up.

And if you do give up, give up with purpose, direction, and ceremony.
